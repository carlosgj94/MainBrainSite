Instead of treating notes as a passive archive, I want a system that turns reading into a maintained body of knowledge. Raw sources stay available, but the useful layer is the curated wiki in between. That is where concepts, connections, revisions, and questions accumulate over time.

Obsidian is a good fit for this because the underlying format is plain markdown and the structure can stay portable. The publishing layer is separate. Only selected notes should become public posts and RSS items, which creates a quality threshold and a useful rereading loop.
