Why RSS Should Be Part of My Thinking Loop

Published 2026-04-06 · Updated 2026-04-06 00:00:00 +0000 UTC

Publishing selected notes into RSS turns the feed into part of a personal rereading and reflection loop.

I want publishing to be part of the way I think, not only the way I share. A note that is clear enough to publish is usually a note that has crossed a quality threshold. It is more coherent, more deliberate, and more worth returning to.

That matters even more if the final reading surface is an ereader. Instead of revisiting notes in a browser or inside the vault, RSS makes it possible to consume selected writing in a calmer environment. That turns the feed into part of the knowledge loop itself.
