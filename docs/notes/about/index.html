What Main Brain is, and why it publishes selected markdown notes.

Main Brain is the public layer of a private Obsidian-based knowledge system.

The private vault holds raw sources, concept notes, synthesis, and working drafts. This site only publishes selected notes that are worth rereading later, especially through RSS.

The goal is not volume. The goal is to make a small stream of durable markdown notes that stay readable across devices and useful over time.
